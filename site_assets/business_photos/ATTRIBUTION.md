# Business photo attribution

Only businesses that have a genuinely free, openly-licensed photograph on Wikimedia
Commons display one. Every other listing has NO photo — the site shows an honest
"the owner hasn't added photos yet" note, and in production the owner supplies their
own photo via the SMS upload flow. We do not use stock photos or any imagery of a
specific business we don't represent.
